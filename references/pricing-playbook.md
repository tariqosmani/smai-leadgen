# Pricing method — moved

The value-based pricing method is per-client now. See
`clients/<active-client>/offer.md` > Pricing method
(e.g. `clients/smart-ai-workspace/offer.md`). The section numbers (§1 discovery,
§2 value model, §3 corridor, §4 three options, §5 milestones, ...) are unchanged.
Resolve the active client from `ACTIVE_CLIENT` in `.env`, or a `client=<slug>`
invocation arg. See CLAUDE.md > Active client.
